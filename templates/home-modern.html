Scam Awareness Tips

Learn the common tactics used by scammers and how to protect yourself

🎣 Phishing Attacks

Scammers impersonate trusted companies via email to steal login credentials. Look for:

  • Generic greetings ("Dear Customer")
  • Urgent language or threats
  • Suspicious sender addresses
  • Links that don't match company domains

💬 Social Engineering

Attackers manipulate you into revealing sensitive information. Be cautious of:

  • Requests for passwords or OTPs
  • Unfamiliar caller IDs claiming authority
  • Pressure to act immediately
  • Too-good-to-be-true offers

🔗 Malicious Links

Dangerous URLs can lead to malware or fake login pages. Watch for:

  • Shortened URLs hiding true destination
  • Misspelled domain names
  • HTTP instead of HTTPS
  • Links in unexpected emails

📱 Mobile Scams

Scammers target mobile users with fake apps and SMS. Protect yourself by:

  • Only installing from official app stores
  • Verifying app permissions
  • Never sharing OTPs via SMS
  • Enabling two-factor authentication

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common security and scam-related questions

What is phishing? ⌄
Phishing is a social engineering attack where scammers impersonate trusted organizations to steal passwords, financial data, and personal information. Attacks typically come via email, SMS, or fake websites that closely mimic legitimate services.
How can I identify a phishing email? ⌄
Check for: suspicious sender address, generic greetings, urgent language, spelling/grammar errors, requests for sensitive information, suspicious links or attachments. Always hover over links to verify the actual URL before clicking.
Is it safe to share OTPs? ⌄
Never share OTPs (One-Time Passwords), PINs, or security codes with anyone, even if they claim to be from your bank or service provider. Legitimate organizations will never ask for this information.
How can I report a scam? ⌄
Report scams to: your bank/service provider, local law enforcement (cybercrime units), national authorities (FBI IC3, NCFR), and the platform where you encountered it. Keep evidence and file a report with our system as well.
